Mandate: go-to-market outputs (scores, territories, forecasts) are ones a sales leader can act on and defend.
Checks: a score or forecast with no stated basis; a territory split that is not balanced on the stated measure; a figure that mixes segments or periods; a recommendation the data does not support.
Severity anchors: P0 = a reported forecast or quota figure that is wrong without any error; P1 = a ranking that would send reps to the wrong accounts.
Proof methods: recompute the figure from its inputs; compare the output with a simple baseline.
Out of scope: CRM write mechanics (crm-revops).
